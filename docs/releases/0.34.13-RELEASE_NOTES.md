# 0.34.13 六项策略优化 / Strategy improvements

- 加牌、删牌时考虑整副牌组效率、费用压力与组合启动估计，改善跳过判断。
- 流派详情显示两回合启动估计，评分解释显示牌组变化。
- 改善已有增幅与可打出后续牌的短组合保留。
- 重新计算出更好的完整路线后，更新已保存路线。
- 减少商店、删牌评分中的重复计算。
- 普通药水结合血量、满栏、Boss 和已知补充手段调整使用取舍。

- Card additions and removals account for whole-deck efficiency, costs and estimated setup reliability, improving skip decisions.
- Details show estimated two-turn setup and the whole-deck score change.
- Better retention of short, playable setup/follow-up combinations.
- Strictly better complete routes can replace saved routes.
- Less repeated computation in shop and removal advice.
- Ordinary potion decisions consider health, inventory, bosses and known replenishment opportunities.
