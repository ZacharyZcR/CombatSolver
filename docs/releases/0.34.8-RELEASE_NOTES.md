# CombatSolver 0.34.8 试玩版

## 简体中文

- 成长／净收益目标下，没有正向目标收益的路线回退到保命与资源比较，不再因收益血量限制拦住完整获胜路线的全自动。
- 有实际收益时仍执行血量限制，保留搜索途中生成成长牌的机会；路线详情解释无收益回退。

## English

- Growth and Net Resources routes without positive objective gains fall back to health and resource comparison. Reward HP limits no longer block full auto for complete winning routes without gains.
- Positive-gain routes still obey HP limits. Searches retain opportunities to generate growth cards, and route details explain the fallback.
