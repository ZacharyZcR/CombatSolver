- 切换偏好后重新计算，保持生存优先与成长/收益限制。
- 当前仅列出已建模方向，不代表完整流派覆盖。

- Select a character-specific mechanism preference in rewards, shops and the combat Growth Strategy panel. Choices are saved per character.

- Changing preferences invalidates old plans while preserving survival priorities and reward limits.
